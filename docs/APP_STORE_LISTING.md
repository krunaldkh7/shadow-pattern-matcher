## Version 1.0

**Promotional text (170)**

> A tangle of gold wire hides a picture in its shadow. Turn it, twist it, and watch 163 shapes come to life on the wall — or draw your own.

**Description (4000)**

> A single spotlight. A tangled sculpture of gold wire. Somewhere in its shadow, a picture is hiding.
>
> Drag to turn the wire, twist with two fingers to spin it, and watch its shadow on the wall. When the shadow matches the dotted outline, the piece clicks into place and glows.
>
> FEATURES
> • 163 hand-picked shapes — animals, objects, letters, numbers and more
> • Five galleries from Easy to Master, each harder and more intricate
> • Draw your own: sketch any picture with your finger and play it as a level
> • Global leaderboard — earn points for every solve and climb the ranks
> • Keep your bulbs: solve without hints for the best score
> • Calm, tactile 3D puzzles with gentle haptics
> • Play offline, anytime
>
> HOW POINTS WORK
> Solve a level for 100 points, earn 50 more for every bulb you keep, and get a speed bonus for solving fast. Only your best result on each level counts.
>
> Stuck? Use a hint to turn the wire halfway, peek at a ghost of the answer, or skip a level and come back later.
